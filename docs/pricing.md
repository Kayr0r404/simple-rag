# Pebble Pricing

The Free plan includes up to 100 notes and 1 device.

The Plus plan costs R4 per month. It includes unlimited notes and up to 5 devices.

The Team plan costs R9 per user per month. It adds shared workspaces and requires a minimum of 3 seats.

Annual billing gives you 2 months free. Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase.

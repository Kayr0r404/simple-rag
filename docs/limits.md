# Limits and Support

The maximum note size is 1 MB.

Attachments are not supported on the Free plan. Plus allows attachments up to 25 MB; Team allows up to 100 MB.

The API rate limit is 60 requests per minute.

Deleted notes stay in the trash for 30 days before permanent deletion.

Contact support at support@pebble.example. Team plan customers get a response within 24 hours.
